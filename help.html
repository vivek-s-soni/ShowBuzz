Booking & Tickets

Step-by-step booking process:
  1. Sign in to your ShowBuzz account
  2. Select a movie, comedy show, or live event
  3. Choose your preferred theatre and date
  4. Select your seats
  5. Proceed to payment and complete the transaction
  6. Your e-ticket will be generated instantly

ShowBuzz accepts the following payment methods:
  • Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Debit Cards
  • Net Banking
  • UPI (Google Pay, Apple Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Digital Wallets

Modification/Cancellation Policy:
  • Bookings can be modified up to 3 hours before the show time
  • Cancellations made 24 hours before the show will receive a 90% refund
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before will receive a 50% refund
  • No refunds on cancellations made less than 1 hour before the show
Contact support for assistance with modifications or cancellations.

E-Ticket Guidelines:
  • Your e-ticket is available immediately after payment confirmation
  • Download and save the PDF or take a screenshot
  • Display the QR code at the venue entrance
  • No need to print the ticket - digital display is sufficient
  • Keep your ticket until after the show for reference

Account & Profile

To create an account:
  1. Click on "Sign Up" on the home page
  2. Enter your full name, email, mobile number, and age

Technical Issues

If you're having login issues:
  1. Verify your email and password are correct
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Try using a different browser
  4. Try on a different device
  5. Contact support if the issue persists

Try these troubleshooting steps:
  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Disable browser extensions/plugins
  • Try a different browser or device
  • Check your internet connection
  • Contact support if problems continue

Don't worry! Transaction reversals are automatic and typically take:
  • Credit/Debit Cards: 3-5 business days
  • Net Banking: 2-3 business days
  • UPI: 1-2 hours
If the amount isn't reversed within the expected timeframe, please contact support with your transaction ID.
