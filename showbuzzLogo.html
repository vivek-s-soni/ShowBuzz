🎟️ ShowBuzz – Smart Event Booking Platform

ShowBuzz is a modern online event booking platform designed to make discovering, booking, and managing events simple, secure, and seamless.

🚀 Key Features:

  • Easy ticket booking with secure payment
  • QR-based ticket verification
  • ShowBuzz Wallet & Cashback rewards
  • PDF ticket download
  • Admin dashboard

🎯 Objective:

To eliminate long queues and provide a smooth digital ticketing experience for users and organizers.
